# Workbench visual smoke checklist

Before merging a public-site presentation change, visually confirm at desktop and mobile widths that:

- the investigation index is reached quickly and the introduction does not dominate the viewport;
- Lab 01 is clearly complete, Lab 02 clearly active, and unbuilt labs do not look complete;
- interactive controls remain readable and keyboard-accessible;
- failure, flexible-boundary, caution, and success colors remain distinguishable without carrying meaning by color alone;
- evidence links remain visible;
- no screenshot, log, identifier, or example introduces non-public data.

Automated checks complement this review; they do not replace it.
