# Public workbench presentation contract

The Field Labs site is a compact index into the repository's engineering investigations. It is not a product landing page and should not use visual scale or decoration to imply evidence that the repository does not contain.

## First viewport

On a typical desktop viewport, the introduction should stay compact enough that the investigation index begins near the fold. The introduction identifies Field Labs, states the reverse-engineering purpose, and points visitors toward the work rather than acting as a marketing hero.

## Visual language

The site keeps the established dark Field Labs palette. Accent colors have semantic jobs:

- cyan: navigation, evidence links, and active technical labels
- violet: flexible or AI-shaped boundaries
- gold: contention, caution, or blocked work
- green: validated or accepted boundaries
- red: reproduced failure states

Decorative effects should remain subordinate to system behavior. Diagrams and motion should explain an investigation rather than fill empty space.

## Evidence boundary

Interactive browser models are explanatory simulations. They do not replace repository tests or imply production behavior. Links to implementation, tests, findings, and limitations remain the evidence path.

All examples are synthetic and must continue to follow `AGENTS.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and `SECURITY.md`.
